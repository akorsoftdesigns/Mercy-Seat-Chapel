Who we are

A mission of The Redeemed Christian Church of God

MERCY SEAT CHAPEL is a mission of The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG). We are bible-believing, multi-racial, loving and family oriented followers of Jesus Christ.

The church was founded in 1952 and since then, the church has grown to over 10,000 parishes worldwide with a total congregational strength of over one million people. The General Overseer of the church is Pastor E.A. Adeboye.

Our Vision

Rooted in Christ. Growing in Faith. Serving with Purpose.

At Mercy Seat Chapel, our vision is to build a Christ-centered family where people encounter God’s mercy and grace, grow as disciples, develop meaningful relationships, and discover their purpose in serving God and others. Five foundational values guide who we are and everything we do.

01

Christ-Centered

Jesus Christ is at the heart of everything we do. Through worship, prayer, and biblical teaching, we seek to honour Him, reflect His love, and live according to His Word.

02

Intentional Discipleship

We are committed to helping people grow into mature followers of Christ through purposeful teaching, mentorship, spiritual development, and opportunities to put their faith into action.

03

Covenant Family Fellowship

We believe church is more than a gathering; it is a spiritual family. We cultivate meaningful relationships built on love, unity, encouragement, accountability, and genuine care for one another.

04

Stewardship & Service

We believe everyone has gifts, talents, and resources entrusted to them by God. We encourage faithful stewardship and joyful service, empowering individuals to contribute meaningfully to the church and wider community.

05

Mission & Outreach

We are called to share the Gospel and demonstrate Christ’s love beyond the walls of the church through evangelism, compassion, community engagement, and practical acts of service.

Our Growth Pillars

Growing Stronger, Together

At Mercy Seat Chapel, growth is about more than numbers. It is about building lasting relationships, reaching new people, helping everyone find a place to belong, and creating opportunities for spiritual transformation. Our approach to growth is anchored in four pillars.

01

Bonding

Building a Caring Church Family

We strengthen relationships through genuine fellowship, prayer, encouragement, celebrating special moments, and supporting one another through life’s different seasons. Through our ministries and departments, we create opportunities for everyone to experience a true sense of belonging.

02

Visibility & Outreach

Sharing the Message of Hope

We extend our reach through personal evangelism, community outreach, digital media, and creative communication. By sharing our faith, church activities, and testimonies, we invite more people to experience the love of Christ and become part of our church family.

03

Integration

Helping Everyone Find Their Place

We believe everyone should have the opportunity to belong, grow, and contribute. Through membership classes, ministry groups, training, and leadership development, we help visitors and members discover meaningful connections and opportunities to serve.

04

Programs

Creating Opportunities for Transformation

Our worship services, teaching programs, conferences, youth gatherings, family events, empowerment initiatives, and community outreach activities provide opportunities to deepen faith, strengthen relationships, discover purpose, and make a difference.

Your Journey at MSC

From Your First Visit to Finding Your Place

Whether you’re visiting for the first time or looking for a church family, we want you to feel welcomed, supported, and connected. Our membership journey is designed to help you discover Mercy Seat Chapel, grow in your relationship with Christ, and find meaningful ways to belong and serve.

  1. 01 Meet

    You’re Welcome Here

    Your journey begins with a warm welcome. Our Welcome & Follow-Up Team is here to connect with you, answer your questions, introduce you to church activities, and help you feel at home.

  2. 02 Decide

    Take the Next Step

    As you get to know our church family, we invite you to learn more about our vision, values, and membership opportunities, helping you prayerfully decide whether Mercy Seat Chapel is where you would like to grow.

  3. 03 Learn

    Discover Who We Are

    Our monthly membership classes introduce you to the church’s beliefs, vision, ministries, and opportunities for spiritual growth. You’ll also learn about SoulCare and the different ways we support our members.

  4. 04 Connect

    Find Your Community

    Through the SoulCare Directorate, we help you connect with a fellowship group or ministry community where you can build friendships, receive encouragement, and grow alongside others.

  5. 05 Serve

    Discover Your Gifts

    Everyone has something valuable to contribute. We encourage members interested in serving to explore ministry opportunities, receive relevant training, and develop their gifts through pathways such as the Workers Academy.

  6. 06 Celebrate

    Grow and Thrive Together

    We celebrate every step of your spiritual journey, from finding a church family to becoming actively involved in ministry. Together, we continue growing in faith, building relationships, and making a difference for Christ.

Pastor’s Message

Raising The Bar

In all sphere of life, and certainly in the Christian life too, we need to be inspired, challenged and given a vision of something better, grander and more challenging. This is obviously true in the sporting world where the phrase “raise the bar” comes into play.

Whether it is the pole vaulter or the high jumper, the bar is raised so that an even greater sporting accomplishment can be attempted. And all great athletes crave this: they don’t want to settle for second best, or that which is easily obtainable. They press on for the best, and in this case, the highest. They are not interested in the ordinary, but want to be the very best in their sport, and raise the bar even higher. They want to lead the way and set the standard. Life is meant to be like that, especially the Christian life.

Christians should not be ordinary or average in any endeavor in life. We have great potential inside us. There are things God wants each of us to do that no one else can do. And through Christ, we have been given everything we need to be successful. However, too many Christians have become satisfied with where they are and never go any further. Some never come close to achieving their potential.

We don’t have to settle for average. Living an average life is like talking on a cell phone when it only has a couple of bars on the screen. It isn’t receiving a very strong signal. The phone is operating, but it cuts in and out or drops calls completely. God wants us to raise the bars in our lives. He has a way for us to live on a level of full bars.

We need to raise the bar. Indeed, with Jesus Christ as our ultimate standard, and his command that we be perfect as our Father in heaven is perfect (Matthew 5:48), every single Christian has to continuously set the bar as high as it can go.
