Privacy Policy

Mercy Seat Chapel · Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mercy Seat Chapel.

At Mercy Seat Chapel (“MSC,” “the Church,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), we value your trust and are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the personal information you share with us.

As a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), we recognize the importance of maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and responsible stewardship of the information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share personal information when you visit our website, participate in church activities, register for programs, make donations, submit prayer requests, apply for employment, or communicate with us through our digital platforms.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have been provided with this Privacy Policy. Where required by applicable law, we will seek your consent for specific uses of your information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Mercy Seat Chapel, we may collect the following categories of information.

A. Personal and Contact Information

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Mailing or residential address
  • City, state, and ZIP code
  • Information voluntarily provided through our contact and registration forms

B. Church Participation Information

When you register for church services, ministries, events, or programs, we may collect:

  • Ministry or program preferences
  • Event registration information
  • Attendance and participation records
  • Volunteer interests
  • First-time visitor information
  • Transportation requests and pickup details
  • Information needed to coordinate church activities

C. Prayer Requests and Pastoral Communication

When you submit prayer requests, testimonies, counseling inquiries, or other personal messages, we may collect the information you choose to disclose.

Such information may include sensitive details relating to your personal circumstances, religious beliefs, family, or well-being.

We recognize the confidential nature of these communications and seek to limit access to authorized individuals responsible for responding to your requests.

Prayer requests and testimonies will not be publicly published without appropriate permission.

D. Donations and Financial Transactions

When you give donations, offerings, or other financial contributions, relevant information may include:

  • Donor name and contact details
  • Donation amount
  • Transaction date
  • Transaction reference
  • Donation purpose or designation
  • Information required for receipts and financial recordkeeping

Where payments are processed through external payment providers, payment information is handled according to their respective privacy and security practices.

We do not require donors to provide payment card details through ordinary contact or inquiry forms.

E. Employment and Volunteer Applications

When you apply for employment or volunteering opportunities, we may collect:

  • Contact information
  • Résumés and cover letters
  • Education and employment history
  • Professional qualifications
  • Responses to job-related screening questions
  • References and other information relevant to the application

This information is used for recruitment, selection, communication, and related administrative purposes.

Where applicable, additional recruitment privacy information may be provided alongside employment application forms.

F. Technical and Website Usage Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website
  • Cookie identifiers
  • Website interaction and performance information

This information may be collected through server logs, cookies, and approved website services.

3. How We Use Your Information

Mercy Seat Chapel uses personal information for legitimate church, administrative, operational, and communication purposes.

These purposes may include:

  • Responding to inquiries, messages, and prayer requests
  • Welcoming and following up with first-time visitors
  • Coordinating worship services, ministries, and church programs
  • Managing event registrations and attendance
  • Providing requested transportation assistance
  • Communicating church announcements and updates
  • Processing donations and issuing acknowledgments
  • Managing employment and volunteer applications
  • Maintaining internal administrative records
  • Improving website functionality and user experience
  • Protecting our website against spam, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We seek to collect only information reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is requested.

4. Sensitive Personal Information

Certain information shared with the Church may be sensitive, particularly information relating to religious beliefs, prayer requests, counseling, health circumstances, or children.

We recognize that this information requires additional care.

Sensitive information will be accessed only by individuals with a legitimate need to process it, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

We will not use sensitive information for unrelated marketing purposes or disclose it publicly without an appropriate legal basis or permission.

Visitors are encouraged not to submit unnecessary sensitive information through general contact forms.

5. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, security, performance, and visitor experience.

These technologies may include:

  • Essential Cookies: Support core website functions, form security, and basic navigation.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand website traffic and improve user experience where analytics services are enabled.
  • Third-Party Media Cookies: May be used when visitors interact with embedded content from video or social media providers.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before activating non-essential tracking technologies.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings and through any cookie-preference controls made available on our website.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

Mercy Seat Chapel does not intend to sell personal information.

We may share limited personal information with trusted third parties where necessary to operate our website or provide requested services.

Such parties may include:

  • Website hosting and technical support providers
  • Payment processors
  • Email and communication platforms
  • Church management and registration platforms
  • Event management providers
  • Authorized professional service providers
  • Relevant authorities where disclosure is legally required

Where appropriate, information may also be shared with authorized church leadership or relevant RCCG administrative bodies for legitimate organizational purposes.

We seek to limit such sharing to what is necessary and to use providers that offer appropriate confidentiality and data protection safeguards.

We do not authorize third-party service providers to use information entrusted to them for unrelated purposes.

7. Children’s Privacy

Mercy Seat Chapel is committed to protecting the privacy, safety, and well-being of children participating in church activities.

Information relating to children may be collected when parents or authorized guardians register them for children’s ministries, classes, or church programs.

Such information may include a child’s name, age group, attendance details, parent or guardian contact information, and relevant participation or safety instructions.

We seek to:

  • Collect only information necessary for children’s programs.
  • Obtain appropriate parental or guardian authorization.
  • Restrict access to authorized ministry personnel.
  • Maintain reasonable safeguards for child registration records.
  • Avoid unnecessary public disclosure of children’s personal information.
  • Obtain appropriate permission before publishing identifiable photographs or recordings of children.

Our general website is not intended to encourage children under 13 to independently submit personal information.

Where applicable children’s privacy laws require additional notices, parental consent, or other safeguards, we will follow those requirements.

Parents or legal guardians may contact the Church to request information about their child’s records or raise privacy concerns.

8. Photography, Video Recording, and Livestreaming

Mercy Seat Chapel may photograph, record, or livestream worship services, church events, and community activities.

These recordings may be shared through our website, official social media pages, livestream platforms, and other church communication channels.

We seek to provide appropriate notice of recording activities and respect applicable privacy and consent requirements.

For testimonials, interviews, individual promotional features, and other situations requiring express authorization, we will seek appropriate permission before publication.

Special care will be taken regarding children’s images and recordings.

If you have a concern about an identifiable image or recording published through our official channels, please contact us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Websites and Social Media

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, video services, donation processors, and other third-party resources.

These services may operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

When you follow an external link or interact with embedded third-party content, the relevant provider may collect information according to its own practices.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external services before sharing personal information.

Mercy Seat Chapel is not responsible for privacy practices controlled independently by third-party website operators.

10. Data Security

We are committed to maintaining reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

Depending on the information and systems involved, these measures may include:

  • Secure website connections
  • Restricted administrative access
  • Access permissions for authorized personnel
  • Website security monitoring
  • Appropriate data storage protections
  • Secure handling of submitted documents
  • System updates and maintenance
  • Backup and recovery procedures

Although we take reasonable precautions, no website, communication platform, or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a data security incident, we will investigate and take appropriate action, including notifications required by applicable law.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, satisfy legitimate administrative needs, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, including:

  • Visitor and contact inquiries
  • Church registration records
  • Event participation records
  • Donation and financial records
  • Employment applications
  • Children’s ministry records
  • Pastoral communications

When information is no longer required, we will seek to securely delete, destroy, or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal requirements and approved retention policies.

12. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, the information involved, and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.
  • Opt out of optional communications.
  • Request information about how your data is used or disclosed.
  • Exercise additional privacy rights available under applicable law.

Certain requests may be subject to identity verification, legal obligations, recordkeeping requirements, or other lawful limitations.

Where applicable law provides rights to opt out of certain data processing, obtain a copy of personal information, or appeal a denied privacy request, we will provide the required procedures.

To submit a privacy request, email info@mercyseatchapel.org.

Please use the subject line Privacy Request and provide enough information for us to understand and verify your request.

We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

13. Email Communications

Mercy Seat Chapel may communicate with you by email, telephone, or other contact methods when you request information, register for activities, or ask to receive church updates.

You may opt out of optional promotional or general church communications at any time using the unsubscribe option where available or by contacting us directly.

Certain administrative communications relating to registrations, donations, or services you have requested may still be necessary.

We do not use employment applications as automatic enrollment into church marketing communications.

14. International Visitors

Our website may be accessed by visitors outside the United States.

When you submit information through our website, it may be processed in the United States or in other locations where approved service providers operate.

Where applicable, we will take appropriate measures to protect personal information transferred across borders.

15. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website, services, data handling practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes or seek consent before introducing new processing activities.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how Mercy Seat Chapel handles personal information, please contact us.
