Become a Member

Membership is more than a name on a list. It’s a commitment to grow in Christ with a church family that knows you, prays with you and walks with you through every season of life. As a member, you’ll receive personal pastoral care, find a place to serve in our ministries and departments, grow through the Believer’s Foundation Academy and Workers Training, and share in the life and vision of Mercy Seat Chapel as we reach our community for Christ.

Your details are private. They are seen only by the pastors and authorized membership administrators.

Questions about membership? Contact the church office at info@mercyseatchapel.org or (301) 740-8118.

Membership Privacy Notice

What we collect and why. Mercy Seat Chapel uses the information in this form to keep its membership records, provide pastoral care, organise ministries and children’s and youth activities, and keep in touch with you about church life.

Sensitive information. Some answers (your faith journey, baptism and, if you choose to give it, your race) are sensitive personal information. We use them only for membership and pastoral purposes and only with your consent below. Race is optional.

Who can see it. Only the pastors and authorized church membership administrators. Where appropriate it may be shared with relevant RCCG administrative bodies for legitimate church purposes. We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

How it is kept. Records are stored securely and kept only while you are a member or as required by law. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by contacting the church office at info@mercyseatchapel.org or (301) 740-8118.

For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.
