Cookie Policy

Mercy Seat Chapel · Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

At Mercy Seat Chapel (“MSC,” “the Church,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), we are committed to providing a secure, accessible, and user-friendly website while respecting your privacy.

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website, www.mercyseatchapel.org.

It also explains the types of cookies that may be used, their purposes, and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

This Cookie Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which provides additional information about how we collect, use, and protect personal information.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.

They help websites function properly, remember user preferences, improve performance, and support certain interactive features.

Cookies may be temporary, lasting only for your browsing session, or persistent, remaining on your device for a specified period or until deleted.

Similar technologies, including browser local storage, may also be used to support website features and preferences.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Depending on the features and services enabled on our website, cookies may fall into the following categories.

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies support essential website functions, security, navigation, and form submissions.

They may be used to:

  • Maintain website security.
  • Protect against spam and unauthorized access.
  • Support contact and registration forms.
  • Maintain essential website functionality.
  • Remember your cookie preferences.

Strictly necessary cookies are generally enabled because they are required for the website to operate correctly.

B. Functional Cookies

Functional cookies help improve your experience by remembering certain choices or supporting interactive features.

They may be used to:

  • Remember website preferences.
  • Support selected interactive features.
  • Maintain user-selected display or accessibility settings.
  • Improve navigation and usability.

Where required by applicable law, non-essential functional cookies will be used only after appropriate consent.

C. Analytics and Performance Cookies

Where enabled, these cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

They may provide information about:

  • Number of website visits.
  • Pages viewed.
  • Time spent on website pages.
  • Website navigation patterns.
  • Technical errors and performance.

This information helps us improve website functionality, identify technical problems, and provide a better visitor experience.

Non-essential analytics cookies will be subject to applicable consent and privacy requirements.

D. Third-Party Media Cookies

Our website may include embedded videos, livestreams, sermons, social media content, and other services provided by third-party platforms.

These may include services such as YouTube and other video or social media providers.

When you interact with embedded content, the provider may place cookies or use similar technologies according to its own privacy practices.

Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent before activating third-party media technologies that are not strictly necessary.

E. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

If Mercy Seat Chapel enables advertising, campaign measurement, or remarketing technologies in the future, those services may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure campaign effectiveness or support advertising.

We will disclose any such technologies and provide applicable consent or opt-out choices before using them.

Advertising cookies are not necessary for basic website functionality.

4. First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

Our website may use two general types of cookies.

  • First-Party Cookies: Cookies placed directly by Mercy Seat Chapel’s website to support website operations and preferences.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Cookies placed by external services integrated into our website, such as video platforms, security providers, analytics services, or approved payment processors.

Third-party providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy practices of external services they choose to use.

5. Cookies Used by Website Features

Certain website functions may involve cookies or similar technologies.

These features may include:

  • Online Forms: Cookies or technical identifiers may support secure form submissions, spam prevention, and session management.
  • Sermons and Livestreams: Embedded media providers may use cookies when visitors interact with video players.
  • Online Giving: External payment platforms may use cookies for transaction security, authentication, and payment processing.
  • Website Security: Security services may use technical identifiers to distinguish legitimate visitors from suspicious or automated activity.
  • Cookie Preferences: A cookie or browser storage entry may remember whether you have accepted, rejected, or customized optional cookies.

The specific cookies used depend on which website features and external services are active.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

We respect your ability to control optional cookies.

Where applicable, our website provides cookie preference controls that allow you to:

  • Accept optional cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Customize cookie categories.
  • Review or update previous choices.

Strictly necessary cookies may remain active because they support essential website functions.

You may also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Most modern browsers allow you to block, restrict, or remove cookies.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website features such as embedded media, form functionality, or saved preferences.

7. Cookie Retention

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their purpose.

  • Session Cookies: Generally expire when your browsing session ends.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain until their configured expiration date or until deleted.

Cookie retention periods may vary depending on the relevant website function or third-party service.

Where applicable, information collected through cookies will be retained in accordance with our Privacy Policy and approved data retention practices.

8. Personal Information and Cookies

Some cookies and similar technologies may collect information that can identify or reasonably be associated with an individual.

This may include an IP address, online identifier, or other device-related information.

Where such information constitutes personal data under applicable law, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and relevant legal requirements.

We seek to limit personal information collection to what is reasonably necessary for legitimate and disclosed purposes.

9. Children’s Privacy

Mercy Seat Chapel is committed to protecting the privacy of children and families.

Our website is intended for general audiences and does not encourage children under 13 to independently provide personal information through website forms.

We seek to implement appropriate safeguards when website features involve children’s information.

Where applicable, we will comply with relevant children’s privacy requirements concerning cookies, online identifiers, parental authorization, and third-party tracking.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have certain rights concerning personal information collected through cookies and similar technologies.

These may include rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information and, where applicable, to opt out of certain processing activities.

Where required, we will also honor applicable browser-based privacy preference signals.

For more information about privacy rights and how to exercise them, please review our Privacy Policy.

You may also contact us with questions about our use of cookies or personal information.

11. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in website functionality, cookie technologies, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain renewed consent for material changes affecting optional cookie technologies.

We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
