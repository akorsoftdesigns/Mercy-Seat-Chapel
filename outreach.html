Our outreach

Sharing His love with the community and the world

Mercy For Many is the outreach arm of the church that helps Mercy Seat Chapel spread God’s word and share His love with the community and the world at large.

We are reaching the world for Christ through outreaches such as our Annual Turkey Drive and Angel Treat. We also visit Seniors in Assisted Living monthly for fellowship. We also make a difference by sponsoring needs of Orphanages around the world.

Christmas

Angel Treat

The joy of Christmas is almost incomplete for the kids without gifts, which sometimes are not affordable to all parents during that season. Mercy Seat Chapel bridges this gap through the Angel Treat to plant smiles on the faces of so many children within the community.

The program creates a memorable Christmas event for kids from Age 0-12, with age-appropriate gifts for every child to go home with. A well-lit and beautiful Christmas scenery that recreates the Christmas story, gives a funfair ambience and a handshake with Santa to complete the package. This also gives the church an opportunity to spread the love of Jesus as the reason for the season through tracts or the word of mouth.

This program has attended to as many as 400 children in one event and has been running for almost a decade.

Thanksgiving

Annual Turkey Drive

Thanksgiving is a season of gratitude, but for many families in our community it can also be a season of need. Through the Annual Turkey Drive, Mercy Seat Chapel shares the blessing of a Thanksgiving meal with families across Gaithersburg and the surrounding communities.

Each year, members and friends of the church come together to give, pack and distribute turkeys and holiday food items to families, seniors and neighbours who could use a helping hand. Every gift is shared with a smile, a word of encouragement and the love of Jesus Christ.

You can be part of the Turkey Drive by giving towards the outreach, volunteering on distribution day, or letting us know about a family who would be blessed by a Thanksgiving meal.

Monthly

Nursing Home Visit

Mercy Seat Chapel has over the years partnered with Interfaith Works to cater for the feeding of the residents at the Homeless shelter in Rockville once in a month. This visit usually includes a time of fellowship, sharing the word, praises and prayer. The residents are served after the session and it is always fulfilling to see them receive the meals with love and gratitude.

There is also a monthly visit to the Montgomery Nursing Home to share fellowship and give hope to the hopeless through the word of God and prayers. Special events are also planned for Easter and Christmas to give them an opportunity to feel the joy and warmth of the season. The Christmas program also includes sharing of Christmas gifts.

The Church also attends to other homes on request and ensures that every contact both the residents and the staff are touched with the love of God.

Worldwide

Orphanage

Through the Mercy For Many Foundation, Mercy Seat Chapel makes a difference by sponsoring needs of orphanages around the world.

Our support helps provide food, clothing, school supplies and other essentials for children who have lost the care of their parents, so that they can grow up knowing they are seen, valued and loved by God. As James 1:27 reminds us, caring for orphans in their distress is at the heart of pure religion.

Members and friends of the church can partner with this outreach through giving and donations of needed items. Contact the church office to learn about current needs.
