Children & Teenagers

Ambassadors of Christ

Mercy Seat Chapel Teen Ministry main purpose is to ground our young teenagers in the way of the Lord, mold new leaders and help bring out the talents that God has placed in them.

Teenagers nowadays are faced with many challenges and temptations that can easily sway them into the sinful ways of the world. The Teen Ministry focuses on practical day to day issues that teenagers encounter such as, peer pressure, obeying their parents, drugs, relationships, self-control, making the right decisions, self esteem in conjunction with providing Biblical principles on how to deal with these issues.

It is the leadership desire to provide dynamic ways in teaching the Word of God. They can range from open discussions, group work, skits, Bible teachings, role play and more. The Teen Ministry has different activities throughout the year to encourage unity and fellowship. Activities consist of, fundraising, trips (Six Flags, bowling, laser tag), outreach programs, food drives, movie nights and many more!

The ministry is growing and is looking for more young vibrant teens to join us as we take a stand for our Lord Jesus Christ.

Young Adults · 18–29

Revive

REVIVE is an integral part of the body of Mercy Seat Chapel that focuses on young adults singles.

We provide a safe place where young adults can serve freely, train leaders, learn and grow in Christ, learn humility in God, self respect and self worth.

We also focus on those who are just entering into their youthfulness and make the church a homely atmosphere for them to worship and serve and also offer various assistance to them that can help evolve their personal lives.

REVIVE consists of a demographic of single youths from the age of 18 to 29.

Women’s Ministry

Pillars of Mercy

The Pillars of Mercy, the women’s ministry-arm of Mercy Seat Chapel, was established with a vision of bringing women together for a godly purpose that includes service, edification for spiritual growth, empowerment, and positive influence in the community.

The group’s name stems from key words from Prov 31:10 and 16, and Heb 4:16. Parts of Prov 31:10 and 16 say “A capable, intelligent, and virtuous woman…, girds herself with strength [spiritual, mental, and physical fitness for her God-given task] and makes her arms strong and firm…” Hebrews 4:16, another very descriptive verse, states “Let us then fearlessly and confidently and boldly draw near to the throne of grace (the throne of God’s unmerited favor to us sinners), that we may receive mercy [for our failures] and find grace to help in good time for every need [appropriate help and well-timed help, coming just when we need it]”.

Put together the words capable, intelligent, strong, and firm, incites another word: “Pillar”. In other words, a woman, who plays the role of a pillar, needs to be capable, intelligent, strong, and firm. As Pillars in the home, church, and community at large, women need to approach God’s presence boldly to receive mercy, and find grace to accomplish their God-given purposes. This group propagates that Mercy Seat Chapel’s women are “Pillars of Mercy”!

Men’s Ministry

Fundamentals

The Fundamentals, the men’s ministry branch of Mercy Seat Chapel, was driven by a vision to bring men together for a noble purpose encompassing service, spiritual growth, empowerment, and positive community influence. The name of this group draws inspiration from the concept of foundational principles.

Combining the attributes of capability, intelligence, strength, and steadfastness embodies the essence of being a “Fundamental.” This term encapsulates the notion that men assuming this role should possess qualities that are capable, intelligent, strong, and resolute. Essentially, men embracing the role of Fundamentals, whether within their families, the church, or the broader community, are called to boldly position themselves for personal growth and community impact. The central message advocated by this group is that the men of Mercy Seat Chapel embody the essence of “The Fundamentals.”

Empowerment

Empowerment

Empowering God’s children for growth, development and excellence.

The mission of the Empowerment Ministry is to seek and connect Mercy Seat family members to educational, job, professional, business and networking opportunities. The ministry will be a conduit for disseminating information to church members who have needs in any of the areas mentioned.

Career and Job Opportunities

The team will search and match church members with available job openings in the metro area. We will also be responsible for ensuring that every member is prepared for job interviews by reviewing résumés and interview role playing. There will be a one-on-one relationship between each team member and the job candidate.

Business Opportunities

The team will play an advisory and supportive role in helping our members looking to start their own business. The team will organize workshops and provide guidance and assistance to members. The team will inform business owners any opportunities that they know will benefit members.

Networking Opportunities

Team members through our personal associations outside the church, will connect our family members with old contacts, friends and associates who are able to help our members with whatever need they may have. This need may include but not limited to access to financial assistance for real estate purchase, access to venture capital, transferring our children to a private school where they have waiting list, referral to private physicians or doctors where they have no new patient intake.
