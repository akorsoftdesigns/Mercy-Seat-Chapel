Children’s Ministry Coordinator

Apply Now Back to Job Openings
The Role

About the Opportunity

Mercy Seat Chapel is seeking a passionate, creative, and highly organized Children’s Ministry Coordinator to support the spiritual growth, development, and well-being of children within our church community.

The successful candidate will oversee the planning, coordination, and delivery of engaging, age-appropriate children’s ministry programs while working closely with church leadership, teachers, volunteers, and parents.

This role requires someone who enjoys working with children, demonstrates strong leadership and communication skills, and is committed to creating a safe, nurturing, and Christ-centered environment where children can learn, grow, and develop their faith.

Responsibilities

What You’ll Do

Children’s Ministry Programs

  • Plan, organize, and coordinate weekly children’s church services, Bible lessons, and age-appropriate activities.
  • Develop engaging learning experiences that encourage spiritual growth and positive character development.
  • Coordinate special children’s programs, holiday events, and ministry activities.
  • Ensure teaching materials and activities align with approved church ministry guidelines.

Volunteer Coordination

  • Recruit, onboard, train, and coordinate children’s ministry volunteers.
  • Develop volunteer schedules and assign responsibilities.
  • Support teachers and volunteers with appropriate resources and guidance.
  • Encourage teamwork, accountability, and continuous improvement.

Child Safety & Welfare

  • Implement and maintain the church’s child protection and safeguarding procedures.
  • Ensure children are appropriately supervised during ministry activities.
  • Maintain secure child check-in and check-out procedures.
  • Follow established incident reporting and emergency response protocols.
  • Protect confidential information concerning children and their families.

Parent & Family Engagement

  • Build positive relationships with parents and guardians.
  • Communicate ministry schedules, activities, and relevant updates.
  • Address parent inquiries and concerns professionally.
  • Encourage family participation in children’s spiritual development.

Administration & Reporting

  • Maintain accurate attendance and program records.
  • Coordinate ministry supplies, learning materials, and resources.
  • Assist with budget planning and resource management.
  • Provide regular updates and activity reports to church leadership.
Qualifications

What We’re Looking For

Required Qualifications

  • Relevant experience working with children in educational, ministry, childcare, or community settings.
  • Strong organizational, leadership, and interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to engage children and parents effectively.
  • Ability to plan and coordinate educational or developmental activities.
  • Demonstrated commitment to child safety, supervision, and safeguarding.
  • Ability to work collaboratively with volunteers, families, and church leadership.
  • Basic proficiency with Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, or similar administrative tools.
  • Ability to meet the position’s scheduling requirements, including Sunday services and relevant ministry events.
  • Ability to satisfy applicable child-safety screening, safeguarding training, and other lawful position requirements.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Education, Child Development, Theology, Christian Education, or a related field.
  • Two or more years of relevant experience in children’s ministry, education, or program coordination.
  • Experience leading volunteer teams.
  • Familiarity with age-appropriate Christian educational materials.
  • Experience planning children’s events and community activities.
  • Current First Aid and CPR certification, or willingness to obtain appropriate training.

Ideal Candidate

We are looking for a compassionate, dependable, energetic, and creative individual who enjoys helping children discover their potential.

The ideal candidate is patient, approachable, organized, and capable of developing meaningful relationships with children, parents, volunteers, and church leadership.
