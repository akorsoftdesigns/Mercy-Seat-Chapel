Executive Assistant

Apply Now Back to Job Openings
The Role

About the Opportunity

Mercy Seat Chapel is seeking a highly organized, proactive, and detail-oriented Executive Assistant to provide professional administrative and operational support to the church’s pastoral and executive leadership.

The successful candidate will play an important role in ensuring the smooth coordination of executive activities, managing schedules, facilitating communication, organizing meetings, and supporting key administrative and ministry initiatives.

This position requires exceptional professionalism, discretion, strong interpersonal skills, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic, faith-centered environment.

Responsibilities

What You’ll Do

Executive & Administrative Support

  • Provide comprehensive administrative assistance to the Senior Pastor and designated church leadership.
  • Manage executive calendars, appointments, meetings, and schedules.
  • Prepare correspondence, presentations, reports, and other official documents.
  • Coordinate administrative tasks and monitor pending actions.
  • Maintain organized electronic and physical filing systems.
  • Anticipate executive administrative needs and provide proactive support.

Communication & Correspondence

  • Serve as a professional point of contact between church leadership, staff, ministry departments, and external stakeholders.
  • Draft and manage official emails, letters, announcements, and communications.
  • Respond to inquiries professionally and direct requests to appropriate personnel.
  • Facilitate timely communication and follow-up on leadership decisions.
  • Maintain professionalism and confidentiality in all interactions.

Meeting & Event Coordination

  • Schedule and coordinate leadership meetings, consultations, and official appointments.
  • Prepare meeting agendas, briefing materials, and minutes.
  • Track action items and ensure timely follow-up.
  • Assist with organizing church conferences, ministry events, and special programs.
  • Coordinate approved travel arrangements, accommodation, and meeting logistics when necessary.

Office & Operational Administration

  • Support the efficient operation of the pastoral and administrative offices.
  • Maintain accurate records, databases, and administrative documentation.
  • Track deadlines, appointments, and operational commitments.
  • Coordinate office supplies, resources, and administrative requirements.
  • Identify opportunities to improve existing administrative processes.

Project & Ministry Support

  • Support the coordination and tracking of strategic church initiatives.
  • Collaborate with ministry heads and administrative teams on assigned projects.
  • Monitor project activities, timelines, and deliverables.
  • Prepare progress updates and administrative reports.
  • Assist with planning and coordinating special programs and community initiatives.

Confidentiality & Professional Conduct

  • Handle sensitive pastoral, personnel, and organizational information with discretion.
  • Maintain strict confidentiality in accordance with church policies and applicable requirements.
  • Exercise sound judgment when managing sensitive communications.
  • Maintain professional boundaries and uphold high standards of integrity and accountability.
Qualifications

What We’re Looking For

Required Qualifications

  • Relevant professional experience in executive assistance, office administration, administrative coordination, or a similar role.
  • Excellent organizational, planning, and time-management skills.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines with minimal supervision.
  • Demonstrated professionalism, integrity, discretion, and confidentiality.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, and common digital productivity tools.
  • Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work effectively with diverse teams.
  • Excellent attention to detail, accuracy, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to maintain accurate records and coordinate complex schedules.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Management, Communications, or a related field.
  • Three or more years of experience providing administrative or executive support.
  • Previous experience supporting senior executives, church leaders, or nonprofit organizations.
  • Experience coordinating meetings, events, and travel arrangements.
  • Familiarity with project management and collaboration tools such as Trello, Asana, or Microsoft Teams.
  • Experience preparing professional reports, presentations, and official correspondence.
  • Knowledge of records management and administrative best practices.
  • Experience working in a fast-paced, service-oriented environment.

Who We’re Looking For

The ideal candidate is a dependable, resourceful, and highly organized professional who takes initiative and demonstrates sound judgment.

They should be comfortable working closely with church leadership, managing sensitive information, coordinating competing priorities, and building positive working relationships with staff, volunteers, and external stakeholders.

We value individuals who demonstrate:

  • Professionalism and integrity
  • Excellent communication
  • Strong organizational skills
  • Initiative and adaptability
  • Confidentiality and discretion
  • Teamwork and collaboration
  • A commitment to excellence and service
