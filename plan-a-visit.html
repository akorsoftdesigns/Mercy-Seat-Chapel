Everything you need to know

What type of church is MSC?

Mercy Seat Chapel is a place to call home for anyone who is seeking to learn more about God and to find their place and purpose in life. We can confidently say that at Mercy Seat Chapel, your Hope will be Restored and your Potential will be Maximized!

When and where are the services?

We hold two weekly services: 10:00am on Sundays and 7:30pm on Tuesdays respectively. The first Sunday of each month is Thanksgiving Sunday, at 10am. All services take place in our main auditorium and are streamed live. We also hold a Holy Communion service on the first Tuesday of each month at 7:30pm.

What should I wear?

Mercy Seat Chapel is a Church for everyone. We encourage people to come as they are and dress in what feels most comfortable.

Is there anything for children?

We love children at Mercy Seat Chapel. From infants to elementary age, we do our best to help your children have a blast while learning about Jesus. We have created a fun and safe environment for each child by implementing a children’s check-in and check-out system. If you’re visiting for the first time you can facilitate your check-in process by pre-registering your child(ren) below.

Registration
Where do I park?

There are several spots to park around Mercy Seat Chapel, however they get filled up pretty quickly. If you’re visiting for the first time our traffic flow staff will assist in getting you a place to park, just let them know you’re a first time guest. There is also a nearby parking garage where you can park and our Mercy Seat Chapel shuttle buses will pick you up from the parking garage and bring you to Church, and then take you back to your car after the service.

Parking Info
Can I get a ride to church?

If you need a ride to Church, simply click below to fill out a Call-A-Ride request form. Please give us at least a 5 day notice so our team can prepare for your pick-up. If you require a recurring pick-up, please indicate so on the form.

You can also call the church on (301) 740-8118 to arrange a ride.
