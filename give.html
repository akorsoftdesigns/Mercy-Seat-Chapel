Why we give

Honour the Lord with your substance

Giving is an act of worship and a response to the mercy and grace God has shown us.

Your generosity helps Mercy Seat Chapel spread God’s word and share His love — through our weekly services and livestreams, our ministries for children, teens, young adults, women and men, and outreaches like Angel Treat, the Annual Turkey Drive, Nursing Home Visits and support for orphanages around the world.

Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.2 Corinthians 9:7
