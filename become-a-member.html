Become Part of Our Family

From Your First Visit to Finding Your Place

Whether it’s your first visit or you’re looking for a church family, here’s how you can belong, grow and serve at Mercy Seat Chapel.

  1. 01 Meet

    You’re Welcome Here

    Our Welcome & Follow-Up Team will greet you, answer your questions and help you feel at home.

  2. 02 Decide

    Take the Next Step

    Learn about our vision, values and membership, and prayerfully decide if MSC is your church home.

  3. 03 Learn

    Discover Who We Are

    Monthly membership classes introduce our beliefs, vision, ministries and how SoulCare supports members.

  4. 04 Connect

    Find Your Community

    Through the SoulCare Directorate, join a fellowship group to build friendships and grow together.

  5. 05 Serve

    Discover Your Gifts

    Explore ministry opportunities, get trained and develop your gifts through pathways like the Workers Academy.

  6. 06 Celebrate

    Grow and Thrive Together

    We celebrate every step as you grow in faith, build relationships and make a difference for Christ.

Ready to Become Part of the MSC Family?

Your journey of faith, fellowship, and purpose starts here. We would love to welcome you, help you connect, and walk alongside you as you grow in Christ.
